# Company Leave Policy

## 1. Leave Types & Allocations
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 10 days per calendar year. Paid. Requires medical proof if > 2 consecutive days.
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 12 days per calendar year. Maximum 3 consecutive days at a time.
- **Paid Time Off (PTO) / Annual Leave**: 15 days per calendar year. Accrued monthly.
- **Unpaid Time Off (Loss of Pay)**: Applicable when paid leave quotas are exhausted.

## 2. Carry Forward Rules
- Up to 5 days of unused Casual/Annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year.
- Unused Sick Leave does not carry forward.

## 3. Approval Workflow
- All leave requests submitted via email are parsed and marked as `PENDING_APPROVAL`.
- HR Manager or Reporting Manager approval is required before the leave is confirmed.
