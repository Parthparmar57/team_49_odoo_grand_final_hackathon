# General Company Handbook & Operations

## 1. Working Hours & Schedules
- Standard Working Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (40 hours per week).
- 1-hour lunch break included.
- Core Collaboration Hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

## 2. Attendance & Regularization
- Check-in and check-out must be logged daily.
- Missing punches can be regularized within 3 business days by contacting HR or submitting via email.

## 3. Probation & Notice Period
- Standard probation period is 3 months from joining date.
- Notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation.
