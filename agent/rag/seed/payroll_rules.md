# Company Payroll Rules & Structure

## 1. Salary Components
- **Basic Pay**: 50% of Total Cost to Company (CTC).
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: 40% of Basic Pay (or 50% for metro locations).
- **Special Allowance**: Balancing component to reach Gross Salary.
- **Provident Fund (PF)**: 12% of Basic Pay deducted as employee contribution.
- **Professional Tax (PT)**: ₹200 per month (standard deduction).

## 2. Payroll Cycle
- Pay period runs from 1st to the last day of each month.
- Payslips are computed on the 28th and distributed after Payrun validation.
- Net Pay = Gross Earnings - (PF + PT + Income Tax Deductions + Unpaid Leave Deductions).
